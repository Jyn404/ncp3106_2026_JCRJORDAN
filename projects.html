Student Projects

Featured

Senior High School Technology & Work Immersion

An inter-school showcase organized by the College of Engineering where aspiring engineers learn about real-world applications of technology.

EN Talks & Seminars

Technical seminars covering emerging trends in technology, such as embedded systems, AI, IoT, cloud computing, and career navigation for future engineers.

Industry & Hackathon Competitions

Participation in major external competitions, such as the Huawei ICT Competition (Cloud and Network tracks) and national tech hackathons.

Senior Project Design & Research Showcases

Culminating capstone presentations and departmental exhibits where senior BSCpE students demonstrate functional hardware-software prototypes, IoT applications, and embedded system designs.

Engineering & College Week Celebrations

Departmental exhibits, laboratory open houses, quiz bees, and student organization activities featured during the annual UE Foundation Week and Campus Week celebrations.
