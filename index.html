Raspberry Pi 3B+ Portable DAP

Embedded Linux · Audio Hardware · v1.5

A battery-powered portable digital audio player built around a headless Raspberry Pi 3B+. Audio runs through a PCM5102A DAC and MAX4410 amp, controlled by a custom Python UI over GPIO, with dual OLED displays and a custom clear PP enclosure housing a flat LiPo cell, charge circuit, and boost converter. Runs roughly 12 hours per charge.
