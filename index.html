BS COMPUTER ENGINEERING · UNIVERSITY OF THE EAST

Where hardware meets software, Engineered for Excellence.

The Computer Engineering (CpE) Program educates students to understand both sides of the hardware-software interface, from designing circuits to creating operating systems. Multidisciplinary in scope, the CpE curriculum integrates the fields of electrical engineering and computer science. This program will uniquely prepare CpE graduates to design and develop embedded digital and computer systems. Graduates with a degree in CpE will be highly skilled and ideally suited for 21st-century industries, including the games industry.

Three reasons students choose CpE at UE

Hardware ↔ Software

Computers rely on hardware and software working together, managed by operating systems that process raw data into usable information and use networking hardware to connect systems from private Intranets to the global Internet.

Hands-on Laboratories

Hands-on laboratories provide students with direct experience using modern facilities—such as digital logic design labs, embedded systems testbeds, and networking suites—to assemble, program, and troubleshoot real-world computer hardware and software systems.

Industry-Ready Careers

Graduates enter high-demand technical roles across tech, defense, automotive, and telecommunications—working as embedded systems engineers, firmware developers, computer hardware engineers, systems architects, network engineers, and full-stack software developers.

What is CpE?

Computer engineering combines electrical engineering and computer science to design hardware components and low-level system software. By integrating physical circuitry with embedded programming, it powers everything from microcontrollers and consumer electronics to high-performance networks and telecommunications.

CpE at UE

The computer engineering program at UE is a balance of engineering science and design. The freshman and sophomore year courses are structured to lay a solid foundation for the junior and senior level design courses by the introduction of courses in hardware, software and the engineering sciences (math, physics, and chemistry).

Specializations

Through practical labs and capstones in embedded systems, logic circuits, microprocessors, and networking, it prepares graduates for careers as embedded software developers, hardware engineers, network administrators, and software developers.

Careers

A computer engineering degree prepares graduates for flexible technical roles in embedded systems, hardware design, software development, and network infrastructure across industries like semiconductors, automotive, telecommunications, and tech.

Faculty

The Computer Engineering faculty at the University of the East brings together experienced educators, industry practitioners, and researchers dedicated to guiding students through hardware-software systems, embedded logic, and network infrastructure. Our instructors mentor students through hands-on laboratory work, research initiatives, and competitive technological showcases.

SCPES

A vibrant and dynamic student organization dedicated to promoting and advancing the field of computer engineering with strong sense of community and culture of excellence.
